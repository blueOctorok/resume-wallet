# Veree Token — Executive Summary

_A simple explanation of how Veree works and why it benefits the business._

---

## What is Veree?

Veree is a **bonus reward token** that drivers earn when they pay for services on our platform (resume verification, premium subscriptions, etc.). It's like airline miles or credit card points—except it can potentially be traded for real money later.

**Key point**: The platform works perfectly fine without the token. It's a bonus, not a requirement.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Driver pays for a service → Earns Veree tokens

| Service              | Cost     | Tokens Earned |
| -------------------- | -------- | ------------- |
| Resume Verification  | $2.99    | ~10 tokens    |
| Premium Subscription | $9.99/mo | ~33 tokens    |

### 2. Tokens accumulate (no trading yet)

Tokens sit in the driver's wallet. They can't sell them yet because there's no market.

### 3. We use revenue to create a trading market (later)

When we're ready, we take a portion of the revenue we've collected and use it to create liquidity on a decentralized exchange (DEX). This gives the tokens a real market price.

### 4. Drivers can now trade their tokens

Early drivers who accumulated tokens can sell them—potentially at a profit if the platform has grown.

---

## The Smooth Decay Model (Aggressive)

We want **scarcity from day one**, so rewards decrease over time using an aggressive decay formula:

```
reward = 10 × (remaining / total)^0.7
```

- **First user**: Earns ~10 tokens
- **At 50% pool used**: Rewards drop to ~6 tokens
- **At 90% pool used**: Rewards drop to ~2 tokens

This is more aggressive than standard decay models, protecting against rapid pool depletion if we get viral attention from crypto communities.

**Why this matters**: Creates strong urgency to join early, while remaining fair (no sudden cliff drops like Bitcoin halvings).

## Why Only Drivers Earn Tokens?

**Employers do NOT earn or receive tokens.** When an employer pays us, those tokens go into a platform fund instead.

**Benefits:**

- Keeps B2B relationships simple (no token accounting for employers)
- No tax/compliance complications for corporate clients
- All rewards flow to the drivers who actually use the platform

| Year   | What Happens                                    |

## Business Benefits

### 1. User Acquisition & Retention

- Drivers want to earn tokens early (urgency)
- Drivers stay on platform to accumulate more
- Word-of-mouth: "I got in early and earned X tokens"

### 2. Revenue Model Enhancement

- Tokens are funded by real revenue (not speculation)
- Treasury can do buybacks to support price
- Trading fees generate additional income

### 3. Marketing & Buzz

- Token price creates free marketing ("Veree up 50%!")
- Crypto communities spread awareness
- Differentiates us from traditional hiring platforms

### 4. No Downside Risk

- Core platform works with or without the token
- If token fails, business continues normally
- Token is additive, not foundational

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **It's a bonus, not the business** — Platform revenue comes from USDC payments, not token speculation

2. **Revenue-backed** — Liquidity is funded by actual business income, making it sustainable

3. **Fair distribution** — Smooth decay rewards early users without creating massive inequality

4. **Employer-friendly** — B2B clients never touch tokens, keeping relationships clean

5. **Founder alignment** — Our tokens are locked/vested, proving long-term commitment
