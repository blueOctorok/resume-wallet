Who Sees What — and How a Shared Card Becomes a New Employer

Three kinds of people touch Provven: candidates (drivers), employers, and the person in between — a potential employer who receives a shared career-card link but doesn't have an account yet. This plan turns that shared card into our best sales tool, without building a third product.

Access Model & Interested-Party Funnel  ·  September 23, 2026  ·  Internal planning document

The Three Audiences

Candidate (driver) No change

Logs in and sees only their own stuff: their blocks (DOT app, CDL, MVR, resume…), their career card, their share link.

Owns their data. Decides what the share link shows.

Potential employer (link viewer) Upgraded

A recruiter or safety manager clicks a shared card link. No account, no login. Sees one verified candidate — and soon, proof there's a whole pool of them plus a 30-second path to employer access.

The shared card is our sales pitch. Every card a driver shares is a demo of the product.

Employer Small change

Logs in and sees the whole talent pool: search, career cards, verified facts, screening orders, outreach.

Gains one new state: "pending approval" for brand-new companies.

The big decision — no third portal. The "show me more candidates like this one" experience already exists: it's the employer portal, and it's what employers pay for. We don't rebuild it for strangers. The shared card teases the pool with counts and gives an instant on-ramp into the portal we already have. Principle: gate the data, not the door — let potential employers in instantly; admin approval controls what they can see, not whether they can enter.

What Stays, What Changes, What's New

Keep as-is
  • Candidate hub + all blocks (DOT app, CDL, MVR, PSP, EV, resume…)
  • Employer portal: talent search, verified facts, screening orders
  • MVR & PSP never shown on public links — stays locked
  • Admin has final say on every new employer (approve / suspend)
  • Connect form for viewers to message the candidate
Change
  • Privacy fix: hide the CDL number on shared cards (keep class, state, endorsements)
  • Pool teaser on shared cards: "One of 47 verified CDL-A drivers within 50 miles — 31 clean MVRs." Counts only, never other drivers' profiles
  • Pending mode in employer portal: a new company sees the one card they came from + pool stats; full search unlocks on approval
Add new
  • "Get employer access" button on every shared card — name, company, work email. 30 seconds
  • Instant pending accounts. Work-email domains get in immediately; free emails (gmail, yahoo) are flagged for manual review
  • Admin queue entries. Approve → full portal. Reject → suspend. Same controls as today, fed by high-intent leads

The Conversion Journey — From Link to Paying Employer

1

Clicks the shared card link

Sees one verified candidate — facts strip, DOT status, CDL class. No login wall. The hook: "this data is real and verified."

2

Sees the pool teaser

"One of 47 verified CDL-A drivers near Columbus — 31 clean MVRs." Numbers only, no profiles. The pitch: "there are more where this came from."

3

Taps "Get employer access"

Name, company, work email. Work domain → instant pending account; free email → manual review first. The close — at peak interest, not a "we'll email you" form.

4

Lands in the employer portal immediately (pending mode)

Full view of the candidate they came for, plus pool stats. No waiting room, no cold lead.

5

Admin approves

Full talent search unlocks. Admin keeps the same approve / suspend authority as today.

Why the signup gate can loosen here. The old problem was drivers picking "employer" on a generic signup screen — ambiguous intent at a shared front door. This funnel is the opposite: the person arrived from a candidate's card, clicked an employer-only button, and entered a company name and work email. Intent is unambiguous — and admin still gates the pool.

Deliberately NOT Building

A third portal or account type Two products, three audiences.
Anonymous candidate browsing The pool is the paid product.
Blurred candidate previews Counts sell the pool without exposing anyone.
MVR / PSP on public links Compliance posture unchanged.
