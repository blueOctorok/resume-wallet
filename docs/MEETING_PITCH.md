# Resume Wallet - Business Pitch

## The Problem

**Hiring is broken.** Companies waste billions on fake resumes, slow payments, and expensive platforms.

- **Fake resumes** cost companies $50+ billion annually
- **LinkedIn charges** 2-3% + $59.99/month per user
- **Payments take** 2-3 days to process
- **No verification** - employers can't trust what they see

## Our Solution

**Resume Wallet** - The first blockchain-powered hiring platform that's faster, cheaper, and more secure than anything that exists.

### What We Built

- **Blockchain verification** - Resumes can't be faked once verified
- **Zero fees** - No transaction fees, no monthly subscriptions
- **Instant payments** - 2-second transactions instead of days
- **AI matching** - Smart resume analysis and job matching

## Why Blockchain?

### Security

- **Unhackable verification** - Once verified, resumes can't be altered
- **Transparent records** - All verification is public and permanent
- **No fraud** - Blockchain prevents fake resumes

### Cost Savings

- **Zero fees** - No transaction fees like competitors
- **Instant payments** - No waiting for bank transfers
- **Global access** - Works anywhere in the world
