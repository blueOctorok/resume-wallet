### Platform Vision Defined

**Ultimate Goal:** Build the AI-powered, blockchain-verified replacement for Indeed/Monster

**Strategic Approach:**

1. Start with CDL drivers (known market, clear verification need)
2. Perfect the AI intelligence and transparency features
3. Expand to all industries with proven platform

### Key Features for Full Platform

- **AI Job Matching:** Real-time compatibility scoring (1-10) for applicants
- **Application Transparency:** Applicants know if/when employers view resumes
- **Employment Verification:** Blockchain-verified work history
- **Employer Intelligence:** AI pre-screens and ranks candidates
- **Resume Optimization:** AI suggests improvements and better-fit jobs

### Technology Decisions Made

1. **Next.js Full-Stack vs Express:** Chosen for AI integration and rapid development
2. **Polygon vs Ethereum:** Low costs enable mass adoption ($0.001 vs $20+ gas)
3. **Hybrid Storage Model:** Best user experience while maintaining blockchain benefits
4. **CDL-First Strategy:** Controlled market to perfect AI before scaling

### Competitive Advantages Identified

- **vs Indeed/Monster:** Verified credentials + AI intelligence + transparency
- **vs LinkedIn:** Direct applications + objective AI scoring + user-owned data
- **Unique Value:** Eliminate application black holes and resume fraud

### Revenue Model Strategy

- **CDL Phase:** Employer subscriptions ($200-500/month) + verification fees
- **Scale Phase:** Freemium model + enterprise tiers + API licensing
- **Long-term:** Transaction fees + AI licensing + workforce analytics
